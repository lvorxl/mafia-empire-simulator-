How to Play

Objective

Lead your college football team to glory by winning games, recruiting top players, and building a dynasty that will be remembered for years to come.

Gameplay

  • Simulate Games: Watch your team compete against opponents each week
  • Recruit Players: Find and recruit talented high school players
  • Practice: Focus training on different aspects to improve team ratings
  • Build Prestige: Win games and championships to increase your program's reputation

Team Ratings

  • Offense: Affects your team's ability to score points
  • Defense: Determines how well you stop opponents
  • Special Teams: Influences field goals, punts, and returns

Winning

Success is measured by wins, conference championships, and national titles. Build your dynasty and become a legendary coach!
